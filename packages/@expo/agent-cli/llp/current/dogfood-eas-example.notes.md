## What worked

- `install` printed the expo-sqlite skill inline. Its `SQLiteProvider` + `onInit` + `PRAGMA user_version` pattern is what I used, and it was right on the first try.
- `status --explain` saw the native change: fingerprint `58914a01` before `install expo-sqlite`, `49ba9e06` after.
- `dev --ios --eas` did build, tunnel, session and open in one command each time (first build 3 min, the next two about 4 to 5 min to open), and it stopped its own session on SIGTERM.

- `runtime:eval` was again the escape hatch: it identified the foreign apps (8) and closed the dev-menu sheet.
